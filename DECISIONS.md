# Implementation decisions

- On 25 September the user approved implementation of Scene 1 after reviewing its storyboard, with an explicit Don't Starve Together style correction. This supersedes the prior concept-only restriction for Scene 1. The new opening uses angular ink cutouts, scratchy shading, muted morning colors and first-person hands. Later proposed scenes remain a separate revision; the portal joins the existing invitation at its gate.
- Scene 1 has four controlled stops: closed door, empty morning doorway, held envelope, open invitation. Six visual beats share four Lanjut actions. The door and reaching hand move together, the camera dips to the threshold, held sprites rise from below, the seal opens into a card, and a generated ink ring grows from the card into a portal. Back reverses to the previous stop. Inactive underlying scenes are inert. The reading stop never auto-advances.
- Seven new PNGs were generated, with real alpha on six cutouts. Near-zero stray alpha pixels were cleaned, the door was trimmed to its silhouette, and assets were resized/quantized without dithering. Image-generated text is avoided: guest name and invitation copy are readable HTML over blank paper. The name uses textContent and the same 60-character limit as the reservation personalization.
- Sound remains gesture-gated and respects mute. Three new procedural cues cover knocking, a wooden door and the portal. Reduced motion removes camera travel and vortex spin while preserving every stop and control.

- The pasted master prompt is the user's request and explicitly adopts the concept document for content. The later PNG instruction supersedes its SVG requirements. Superseded SVG artwork and generators were removed.
- Twenty-nine PNG artworks use the supplied Biff references: angular ink, crosshatching, slender adult figures, parchment and maroon. The groom retains recognizable hair and round glasses. The original adult bride is a storybook avatar, not an asserted photographic likeness.
- The generator baked neutral checkerboards into its output, including a transparency retry. The user explicitly authorized code cleanup. Bright neutral background pixels were removed while retaining warm fills and dark strokes, then reviewed on ink and paper.
- The 24 September direction restored the dark palette and replaced the scrolling page with a visual novel. Those 16 full-screen scenes remain after the new opening, including one scene per rundown item. Only Kembali/Lanjut navigate the story. Business actions such as submitting, opening Maps, saving a ticket and music remain separate controls. Final Lanjut and the first opening's Kembali are disabled.
- Named HTML layers and clipped image copies replace SVG internal IDs. Gate leaves hinge, chains fall, and CSS perspective/GSAP Z transforms move near and far PNG layers at different rates. The first transition passes through the open gate. Reverse navigation reverses travel; repeated clicks are locked during a transition. Dialogue fades in after arrival. No scroll, wheel, swipe, or menu is required for navigation.
- CSS and procedural assets use the six palette tokens. Generated paintings contain tonal texture in the same color family. The old 30 KB SVG limit was superseded; PNGs are resized, quantized and lazy-loaded. A few complete alternate kit assets are included but not loaded by the invitation.
- GSAP controls transform/opacity. Only the current scene runs ambient loops; page visibility pauses them. Reduced-motion mode removes repeated movement, travel and parallax. Inactive scenes are inert and hidden from assistive technology. Only the current and neighboring scenes preload artwork. No displacement or SVG filter is used.
- The viewport uses dynamic height and safe-area offsets. Dialogue and navigation stay within the screen. Only the reservation/ticket dialogue may scroll internally on constrained heights or while a keyboard is open. Admin retains normal document scrolling.
- Three bright storybook assets were generated during the earlier requested direction. They are retained as source alternatives but are not loaded by the final dark visual novel.
- The Vite project lives in `invitation/` to preserve the supplied concept and character references. No analytics, external font requests, stock assets, or downloaded music are used.
- Supplied Indonesian copy and event facts are retained. Necessary accessibility and admin operational labels use concise Indonesian. There is no invented address, family-lineage section, capacity state, or photo-app link.
- All changeable event details, rundown, guest limit, URLs and music live in `src/config.js`. The build synchronizes the Apps Script guest-limit constant. Calendar times use the exact UTC equivalents of 09:30–13:40 WIB.
- Storage is exclusively the owner's Google Sheet. No account-authorized endpoint was supplied, so `APPS_SCRIPT_URL` remains empty and submission reports failure. No fake successful reservations ship. Backend code and owner setup are complete; live Google behavior remains unverified.
- Same normalized names intentionally identify the same reservation as requested. Different guests with identical names require staff resolution; no additional identity field is introduced.
- Every backend write is locked. The PIN is generated/stored in Script Properties and checked server-side. Formula-leading names and normalized keys are stored literally. The test PIN appears only in test files, never in the browser bundle.
- QR payloads contain only reservation IDs. Local storage supports ticket recall. Downloaded tickets use Canvas at 900 × 1430 with a high-contrast QR. Tests use the same QR decoder as admin.
- Web Audio generates a periodic 60-second ambient buffer plus nine effects, including three Scene 1 cues. A single `BGM_SRC` value supports final music replacement.
- The Sites preview remains owner-private. Vercel-compatible static output is supplied for guest deployment. `BASE_URL` initially points to the preview and should change to the final guest domain.
- The verification report distinguishes local browser results from live Google deployment, true concurrent Google writes, physical camera quality, mid-range-phone frame rate, and private-link preview access, which require owner/hardware checks.
